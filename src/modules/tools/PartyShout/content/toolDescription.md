The **Party Shout** tool let's you send a message to your party that tags everyone so they will receive a notification. This is useful for announcements that your party needs to see, or just to get everyone's attention.
Just like regular Habitica messages, Party Shouts also support [Habitica's Markdown](https://github.com/HabitRPG/habitica/wiki/Markdown-in-Habitica).

**Note:** Only the leader of the party can use the Party Shout tool.
