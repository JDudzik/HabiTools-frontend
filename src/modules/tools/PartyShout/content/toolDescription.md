The **Party Shout** tool let's you send a message to your party that tags every so they will receive a notification. This is useful for announcements that you want the highest odds of everyone seeing, or just to get everyone's attention in the party.
Just like regular Habitica messages, Party Shouts also support [Habitica's Markdown](https://github.com/HabitRPG/habitica/wiki/Markdown-in-Habitica).

**Note:** Only the leader of the party can use the Party Shout tool.

Example of a Party Shout Message:
> This is an example of a party **shout** message!
> 
> ---
> *[Party shout via HabiTools](https://habitools.online/)*
> *[@member]() [@member]() [@member]() [@member]() [@member]()*
