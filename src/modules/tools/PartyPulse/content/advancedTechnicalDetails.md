There are a total of 7 tiers (not including calibration). From highest to lowest the tiers are:
*Paragon*, *Active*, *Passive*, *Coasting*, *Slowing*, *Disengaged*, *Dormant*.

During calibration, points are doubled to quickly estimate their tier.
The tier is derived from the member's current score and used to color their card.

**When a pulse occurs, it increases or decreases points based on these factors:**
- Did the member log-in at least once since the last pulse (24 hours)?
- Did the member receive a loot-drop within the last 48 hours?

**Some additional nuances:**
- Because of how Habitica keeps track of log-ins and how Party Pulse checks this data, sometimes a member's score will jump up or down more than expected, but it will even-out the next day.
- Because loot-drops are technically random, we validate for 48hrs instead of 24hrs. This is to compensate for the fact that occasionally a member may have been active, but may not have received an item drop.
