The **Party Pulse** tool tracks your party members' recent Habitica activity and assigns each member an activity score. Party Pulse updates every 24 hours.
Every member will also be assigned a tier based on their current activity level, but there is a 7-day calibration for each member before they're given a tier.

Scores are capped at +42 and -42 points. (For specifics on point accrual, open The Technical Details section).
