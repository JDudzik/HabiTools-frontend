## Auto Accept Quests

Automatically accept quest invitations from your party members in Habitica. When a party member invites you to a quest, this tool will accept it on your behalf—no more missing quests because you were away or forgot to check.

### How it helps

- **Never miss a quest** — Invitations are accepted instantly when your party members send them
- **Seamless gameplay** — Stay focused on your questing and dailies without manual steps
- **Reliable automation** — Runs via webhook (instant) and backs up with hourly checks to ensure nothing is missed

### What you need

- A linked Habitica account
- An active party with quest members
- This tool enabled on your HabiTools account
