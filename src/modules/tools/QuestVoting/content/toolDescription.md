#### **The Quest Voting tool changes how your party picks its next quest!**
Your party now votes on what to play next:
1. A ballot with 4 randomly chosen quests is posted to the party chat.
2. Party members vote for the quest they want.
3. When the current quest ends, the quest with the most votes starts **automatically**.
4. A new ballot is then posted for the follow-up quest, and the cycle repeats.

#### **Quest options come from volunteers:**
Any member of the party can volunteer to add their inventory to the roster of available quests by enabling this tool.
Each volunteering member can set individual filters, so only quest categories of their choosing will be added to the roster.

#### **Everyone gets to vote:**
Everyone in the party is allowed to vote, even if they don't enable the Quest Voting tool. By default, _Secure Mode_ is enabled which requires everyone voting to link their Habitica account. If you don't want this requirement, the party leader can disable _Secure Mode_, which makes it so anyone can vote an unlimited number of times (only do this if you trust your members to not manipulate ballots).

#### **Party-leader controls Quest Voting:**
Quest Voting will only officially start for the party when the party-leader activates the tool. Additionally, The Quest Voting tool will automatically send important messages, ballots, and results to the party chat via the party leader.
