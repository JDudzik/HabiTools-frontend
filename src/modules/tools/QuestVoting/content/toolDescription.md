**Quest Voting overhauls how your party will choose quests!**
It works by openning a ballot in your party-chat with quests for your party to vote for. After the currently running quest ends, the option with the most votes will automatically be opened, and the cycle repeats with a new ballot for the follow-up quest.

Quest options are collected from the inventories all of your party members who have enabled this tool. Only members who enable this tool, and thus agree to participate, will have their quests added to the roster. Each member can also set individual filters, so only quest categories of their choosing will be added to the roster.

The party leader will send automated messages to the party-chat with Quest Voting updates.
