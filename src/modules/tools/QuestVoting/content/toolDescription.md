Quest Voting automates picking your party's next quest.

How it works:
- Party members enable the tool and contribute their eligible quests.
- When ballots open, party chat receives 3 quest options plus an optional "Other Random" option.
- Anyone in the party can vote from the link.
- When the current quest ends, the system attempts to open the winning quest immediately.

Only the party leader can fully run party-wide automation. Other members can still join the roster and prepare their filters.
