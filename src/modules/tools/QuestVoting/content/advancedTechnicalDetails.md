**Who hosts the chosen quest?**
When a quest wins the ballot, someone has to be the player who actually hosts it. To decide who, first, the Quest Voting tool checks who's eligible to host it (ie: they own it and their filters permit it), and then chooses using a weighted random draw based on how many copies each player owns. So a player who owns more copies of the quest is slightly more likely to be the host of the quest.

**How ballot options are picked:**
First, the tool collects all quests that are eligible to be hosted by the volunteers based on filters and settings. It then chooses a random option from that list. However, quests that were chosen within the last 3 ballots won't be options.

**Dealing with Ties and no-votes:**
Whenever there is a tie between quest options when the ballot ends, one option is randomly picked between the highest-voted options. This includes the scenario where a ballot didn't receive any votes at all.
