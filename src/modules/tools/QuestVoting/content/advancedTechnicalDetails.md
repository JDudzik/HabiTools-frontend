Technical details summary:

- Party-wide state is saved once per Habitica party.
- Personal filters are applied per participant and only affect future ballots.
- In secure voting mode, voters must be logged in, linked to Habitica, and currently in the party.
- In insecure mode, anonymous votes are allowed and tracked without a Habitica ID.
- Ballots stay open until the current quest ends.
- If a winning quest cannot be hosted, the system retries hosts and can fall back to runner-up options.
- Quest launch weighting reduces repeat-host bias from recent selections while still considering quest inventory counts.
