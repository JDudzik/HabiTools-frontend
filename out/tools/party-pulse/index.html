Party Pulse

The Party Pulse tool tracks your party members' recent Habitica activity and assigns each member an activity score. Party Pulse updates every 24 hours. Every member will also be assigned a tier based on their current activity level, but there is a 7-day calibration for each member before they're given a tier.

 

Party pulse will also show you: 1: How many days each member has spent asleep in the inn over a rolling 2-week basis. 2: A chart showing the history of each member's score (after 3 updates).

 

Scores are capped at +42 and -42 points. (For specifics on point accrual, open The Technical Details section).

There are a total of 7 tiers (not including calibration). From highest to lowest the tiers are: Paragon, Active, Passive, Coasting, Slowing, Disengaged, Dormant.

 

During calibration, points are doubled to quickly estimate their tier. The tier is derived from the member's current score and used to color their card.

 

When a pulse occurs, it increases or decreases points based on these factors:

  • Did the member log-in at least once since the last pulse (24 hours)?
  • Did the member receive a loot-drop within the last 48 hours?

 

Some additional nuances:

  • Because of how Habitica keeps track of log-ins and how Party Pulse checks this data, sometimes a member's score will jump up or down more than expected, but it will even-out the next day.
  • Because loot-drops are technically random, we validate for 48hrs instead of 24hrs. This is to compensate for the fact that occasionally a member may have been active, but may not have received an item drop.
